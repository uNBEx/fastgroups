# FastGroups

**Raid group sorting for raid leaders.** See your raid as raid-frame cards, drag players
between groups, split the raid into balanced halves with one click, and save the setup for
next time.

Only the raid leader (or the assistant who arranges groups) needs FastGroups. Nobody else in
the raid has to install anything.

## Why

Players land in raid groups in join order. Many bosses need an equal split, players
learn their side from their group number, and healers see raid frames sorted by group.
FastGroups makes that setup fast and repeatable.

This is also very useful for PUGs, as you can quickly set up the groups even when the squad is constantly changing from boss to boss.

## Features

- **Group board**: class-colored cards with spec, role and a melee/ranged badge. Drag onto an
  empty slot to move, onto a player to swap.
- **Balanced halves**: odd/even (1/3/5 vs 2/4/6) or low/high groups, with tank, healer, melee
  and ranged counters per half. Or plain groups without halves.
- **Auto-split**: balances roles and classes between the halves while moving as few players as
  possible.
- **Draft, then Apply**: nothing changes in the raid until you press Apply.
- **Mythic aware**: only groups 1-4 count; groups 5-8 are the bench.
- **Loadouts**: save setups per boss. Loading one shows who is missing, new or returning, and
  **Auto-fill** puts substitutes into the empty slots.
- **Rosters**: plan groups from your guild before the raid forms.
- **Sharing**: export strings, or send a loadout in game to another FastGroups user.
- **Specs** from BigWigs / LibSpecialization broadcasts and quiet, paced inspects.

## Usage

- `/fg` opens the window. `/fg demo` shows a fake raid to try everything solo.
- Minimap button and addon compartment entry.

Typical raid night: open FastGroups, click the loadout for the boss, press **Auto-fill** if
anyone is missing, then **Apply**.
